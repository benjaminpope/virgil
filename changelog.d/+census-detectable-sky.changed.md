The orbit census (`scripts/orbit_population.py`) gates failures and misses only in an a priori detectable stratum (true period Nyquist-sampled by the within-season night cadence, and the true orbit's expected Δχ² over the best static model on the inlier nights above the 1 % false-alarm threshold), and scores retrieval in the sky plane (rank and 99 % miss of the time-averaged squared distance from the posterior-mean track). New columns `detectable`, `nyquist_ok`, `dchi2_true`, `dchi2_thresh`, `pit_sky`, `sky_miss`; the element-space lines stay as report-only. Criteria in `design/orbit_census_criteria.md`.
