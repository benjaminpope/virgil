- Pipeline checks that could not fail now can. `alias_ambiguity` fails when every rival alias band crashed (`n_failed`) instead of reporting a single band; `bands_failed` fails when at least half the bands were lost (`n_bands`); `winner_flags` fails on `no-valid-mode`; the disk `laplace_pd` check fails on a non-positive-definite Laplace Hessian, which invalidates the reported errors. `chi2_reduced` and `error_scale` take `n` and widen their warn/fail limits to the χ² tail bounds at 1e-3 and 1e-6 (the fixed 2 and 5 stay as the floor; `chi2_limits` exposes the bounds). Without `n` the verdicts are unchanged.
