`DiskPipeline`: a geometry-ring parameter that ends on its prior bound (typically the radius on the `r_in` hole) is now reported by the `at_bound` check as `geometry.<name>`. When the full Hessian there is not positive definite (an active bound), the parameter is held fixed for the Laplace widths of the others, the standard treatment of an active bound, and no longer fails `laplace_pd` as a missing width. A missing width of a free parameter still fails.
