`Image(rotation_deg="grid")` (provisional) follows the data's uv lattice, as for `FlaredDisk*`. `starting_image` and `clean` default to it (resolved to the lattice angle; `CleanResult.rotation_deg` records it, and `restored` turns the beam), so imaging fits on rotated AMI/DISCO lattices take the exact matrix transform instead of the per-point DFT. The pixel arrays of their results are then in the lattice frame, not North up: `plot_model` and the metrics (`resample`, scores) render a rotated `Image` as the sky, which resamples it bilinearly, so scores on such images can differ slightly from before. `dirty_image` and `residual_image` take `rotation_deg` (default 0, `"grid"` allowed). Data without a lattice are unchanged
