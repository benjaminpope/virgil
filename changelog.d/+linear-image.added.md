`virgil.models.LinearImage` (Provisional): pixel image whose free array is the pixel flux (`parametrisation="linear"`, bounds from `bounds()`) or its square root (`"sqrt"`, unbounded); total flux is the sum of the pixels. Works with the radio χ², the Toeplitz term and the regularisers (acting on pixels over a fixed `reference_flux`); `MaxEntropy` uses Skilling's un-normalised form and requires `"sqrt"`.
