Two float32 fixes. Grid fits (`optimized_flux_grid` and friends) no longer report a flux as unconverged at a point that fits almost perfectly: this was a regression of 0.4.2's change of the convergence criterion to −½χ² (#750), whose rounding bound ignored the rounding error of the residuals themselves; the check now includes it, ε·√(2|ℓ|)·rms((|model|+|data|)/σ). The star pipeline's shape-constraint gate (`shape_ratio`, `constrained`) now computes its Fisher matrix in float64, so its ratios no longer depend on the platform's float32 rounding and can differ by a few percent from earlier versions.
