Grid fits (`optimized_flux_grid` and friends) no longer report a flux as unconverged at a point that fits almost perfectly in float32, where the rounding noise of the log likelihood far exceeds the bound for the sum of squares; the convergence check now allows for that measured noise. The star pipeline's shape-constraint gate computes its Fisher matrix in float64, so its ratios no longer depend on the platform's float32 rounding.
