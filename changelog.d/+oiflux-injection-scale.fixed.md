`injection_limits` on `OI_FLUX` (and correlated-flux) data was wrong whenever the grey-scale prior mean `mu` differed from the data's own scale `k`: the companion's spectral signal was injected at `mu` instead of `k`. In a test scene the limit was 0.031 at `mu = k`, 0.091 at `mu = k/2` and clipped to the search bound (1.0) at `mu = k/3`. The null and companion models are now evaluated at the null fit's grey scale, so the limits no longer depend on `mu` or the data's units.
