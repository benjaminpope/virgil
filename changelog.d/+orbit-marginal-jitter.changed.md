`position_cells` marginalizes a between-night astrometric jitter (`jitter=None`, the new default): one offset of each night's position of variance σ_J² (none on the velocity), σ_J one more chart coordinate, log-uniform on 1 µas to 3 mas, replacing the type-II-ML `fit_jitter`. `reference_jitter` seeds the search, `fit_chi2` reports raw χ²/N on the quoted and on σ² + σ_J² errors, `CellsResult.jitter` is the posterior median (the unweighted draws' where a cell failed, NaN with none), and the census gains a jitter arm (`scripts/orbit_population.py --jitter`).
