Adds `virgil.orbit_visibility` (provisional): the visibility-level leg of the alias-resolved orbit fit. `outlier_exact` integrates each night's per-exposure likelihood (within-night motion, its own error scales, gains and offsets marginalized) against the joint prior predictive of the night state by adaptive Genz–Malik cubature in 3 to 5 dimensions, `visibility_leg` bridges from the position leg with `virgil.smc` under one global error scale per block, `swap_label` and `report_invariants` handle the brighter/fainter and antipodal label, and `fit_cells` runs the whole path on prepared data. Not yet wired into `OrbitPipeline`.
