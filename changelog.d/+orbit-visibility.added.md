Adds `virgil.orbit_visibility` (provisional): the visibility-level leg of the alias-resolved orbit fit. `outlier_exact` integrates each night's per-exposure likelihood (within-night motion, its own error scales, gains and offsets marginalized) against the joint prior predictive of the night state by adaptive Genz–Malik cubature in 3 to 5 dimensions. `visibility_leg` refines each position-level mode by Newton on the per-exposure target, builds a defensive mixture on the refined modes and their symmetry images, estimates `ln Z` by importance sampling and draws by HMC resample-move (no tempering); `visibility_cells` runs it in every cell the position leg keeps. `swap_label` and `report_invariants` handle the brighter/fainter and antipodal label, and `fit_cells` runs the whole path on prepared data. Not yet wired into `OrbitPipeline`.
