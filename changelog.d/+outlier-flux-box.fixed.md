The orbit-cell outlier density `b_k` integrates the uniform flux prior over the Gaussian `ln f` measurement error (`OutlierPrior.log_flux_box`, closed form with normal CDFs), so a night with an observed `ln f > 0` has a finite `b_k` instead of `-inf` and a `ValueError` in `position_leg`. The population census no longer truncates the simulated `ln f` noise, and it scores ω folded mod 180° so near-twins are right up to the orbit swap (ω → ω + π), enforced by a test.
