Every orbit fit (`fit_orbit_aliases`, `OrbitPipeline`) now carries one night-outlier mixture with a marginalized rate (Beta(½, ½), exact: `virgil.likelihood.group_outlier_mixture`), in the search, the modes, the no-orbit null and the posterior alike. The new `outlier` setting picks its component: `"mixture"` (a companion anywhere in the prior's disc; the default, the nearest continuation of the old behaviour, not chosen on merit) or `"inflation"` (the night's model at an inflated noise scale, needs `s_max`). Showcase results can change, and can get worse: the plain-likelihood search, the polish step and the heuristic night drop are gone.
