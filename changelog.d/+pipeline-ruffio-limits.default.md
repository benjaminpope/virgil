`BinaryPipeline` contrast limits (`limits` stage) now default to Ruffio et al. (2018): the search's best flux and Laplace σ on the quoted errors, a Gaussian truncated to flux ≥ 0, at the `norm.cdf(sigma)` quantile. The contrast-limits tutorial's deepest limit goes from 9.69 to 10.05 mag. `limit_method="absil"` restores the Absil et al. (2011) chi-squared limits; `summary["limits"]["method"]` and the `grids.h5` meta record which was used.
