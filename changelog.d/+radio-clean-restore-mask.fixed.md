CLEAN restore and auto-mask outputs change (audit 724#11.1, 727#4.1). `restore` centred its kernel half a pixel off on even-sized images, shifting every restored component by (0.5, 0.5) pixels and lowering its peak by about 4 per cent; the kernel now sits on pixel `n // 2`, so odd sizes (all that `clean` accepts) are unchanged. `usemask="auto-multithresh"` now follows Kepley et al. (2020) for its seed threshold: the noise term is `noisethreshold × rms + median` of the residual itself (it was the rms of the beam-smoothed residual) and is never capped, so source-free noise maps no longer seed masks. The sidelobe term stays capped at half the peak (virgil#551). Masks, and hence components, can change on any `auto-multithresh` run.
