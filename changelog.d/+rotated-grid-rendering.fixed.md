Rendered images change on rotated grids only (`rotation_deg="grid"` or a numeric `rotation_deg` other than 0, and rotated-lattice data): `GravityDarkenedStar` rendered an unrotated, shifted and shrunk star on a rotated grid (a point at ±90°), and the in-plane blur of `ModulatedGaussianRim` and `MultipoleDisk` ignored the grid rotation; both now follow the lattice, and the visibilities of those fits on rotated lattices change accordingly. Unrotated grids are bit-identical.
