**Breaking:** the weights of `TSV` and `TV` are now dimensionless, normalised as in eht-imaging (`norm_reg=True`): `TSV` is divided by `F² (p/B)⁴` and `TV` by `F p/B`, with `F` the image flux (1), `p` the pixel scale and `B` a beam, so a weight means the same at any grid size, flux or support. `B` is the new `beam_mas` argument, by default the field of view `√(npix) p`. Before, the penalty acted on unit-sum pixels, so on a large grid it was negligible against χ²: a `TSV` weight swept over four decades changed nothing. An old weight `w` is reproduced exactly by the new weight `w` with `beam_mas` set to the pixel scale (`p/B = 1`), or by `w (p/B)⁴` (`TSV`) or `w p/B` (`TV`) with the default beam. `TV`'s `epsilon` is now relative to the image's flux.
