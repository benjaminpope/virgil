Orbit cells: the predicted positions solve Kepler's equation in the eccentricity vector `k + ih = ζ²` (Broucke & Cefola 1972) instead of through the anchor mean anomaly `M_a = arg ζ²`, so their derivatives are exact at `e = 0`. At a near-circular mode (`ζ ≈ 0`) the old autodiff Hessian in `ζ` was rounding noise of order 10–100, platform dependent, so the Laplace covariances were too narrow there and the defensive importance sampling missed mass (the intermittent `test_the_position_evidence_of_a_tiny_case_matches_prior_importance` failure).
