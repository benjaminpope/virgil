`fit_orbit_aliases` normalizes each night's block residuals by its empirical block scale before pooling in `_temper_factors` (and whitens 4+ station closure phases via `cp.basis`), scales the default `min_distance_mas` to `min(0.5, 0.5 * resolution_mas)` when `min_distance_mas=None`, wraps periodic angles in `_Proposal.log_pdf`, truncates the Laplace fallback at prior bounds by the Gaussian in-bounds probability `p_in` when `ess < ess_min`, and `OrbitPipeline`'s single-star null (`_null_verdict`) includes the 1-D Laplace Occam factor under the log-uniform primary-diameter prior when `primary_diam` is fitted.
