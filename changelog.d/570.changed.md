Results change by default only in these places: `variational` runs `regularizers` and `likelihoods` in the requested precision (float64 by default); `gauss_newton_mass` and the dense SVI start include the gain, closure-offset and centred `PopulationScale` curvature; and fits whose isotropic-prior parameter sat at a pole no longer warn about being at a bound. The closure-phase likelihood is unchanged: the periodic penalty stays whitened by the per-element errors only.
