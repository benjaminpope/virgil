`AngleVector` priors keep the dtype of their input (no silent float32/float64 mixing under `fit(dtype=...)`). `variational` casts `regularizers` and `likelihoods` to `dtype` with the rest of the problem, so float64 SVI (the default) no longer evaluates them in float32. The Gauss–Newton curvature rows used for `gauss_newton_mass` and the LM/preconditioning curvature now include the fitted gain and closure-offset mode rows and the centred `PopulationScale` rows, and hold the noise terms fixed under differentiation. `at_bound` no longer flags the poles of `IsotropicInclination` and `IsotropicLatitude` as prior bounds, though a truncated range's other end still counts. The `fit` docstring describes `post_step` as acting on the unconstrained coordinates.
