`_unit_orbit` no longer returns NaN at the parabolic boundary: eccentricity is clamped to `1 - eps` and `1 + e cos f` floored at `eps`, so positions and gradients stay finite at `e = 1`. The `FlaredDisk` docstring now notes its `sin i` surface projection versus the exact `tan i` of `MultipoleDisk` with `PowerLawHeight`.
