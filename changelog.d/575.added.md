`imaging.log_evidence`, `imaging.error_scale` and `imaging.laplace_samples` take `priors=` and `regularizers=`. A free parameter in `priors` (say `env.flux ~ LogUniform`) enters the Laplace evidence through its log prior density at the MAP and its Schur-complement block of `JᵀJ`, not as a unit-Gaussian latent, so the result is invariant to its units; a probabilistic regularizer (`Centroid`) enters `χ²` and `J`. Without them the three functions are unchanged.
