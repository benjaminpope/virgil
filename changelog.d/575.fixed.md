`imaging.log_evidence`, `imaging.error_scale` and `imaging.laplace_samples` now accept `priors=` and `regularizers=` (and `ensemble.evidence_grid` forwards its fit priors and regularizers to `log_evidence`), so free non-latent parameters such as `env.flux` and probabilistic regularizers (`Centroid`) enter the Laplace evidence, effective degrees of freedom and posterior covariance through their prior density and `JᵀJ` Schur complement; `ensemble.combine` also tests the 180° point-inverted image when registering `star=False` members on datasets without phase observables (`phi` or differential phases); `limits.absil_limits` and `limits.injection_limits` order the null parameter vector with `ordered_values` regardless of the flux key's position in `grid`; `detection.detection_statistics` weights `log_bayes_factor` by 1-D trapezoid differentials along each coordinate axis (and in `log flux` on a positive flux axis); and `pipeline.StarPipeline` evaluates its posterior sampler checks on the selected model while downgrading sampler issues on non-selected comparison models to warnings.
