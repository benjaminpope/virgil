`ImagingPipeline(grid_growths=None)` now follows the engine's 3 growths (was 2), so sigma maps can change. `BinaryPipeline` `flux_range` defaults to `[1e-5, 1.0]` (was `[1e-5, 0.5]`, which excluded companions brighter than half the primary): the search grid, the log-uniform flux prior and the grid-averaged `log_bayes_factor` change with it (about -0.06 nats for an unchanged faint detection), and with `n_flux` fixed the log step grows by about 6%.
