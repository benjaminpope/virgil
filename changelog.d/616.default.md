`BinaryPipeline` `global_nsigma` (and so the `detection` check) is now the significance of the grid maximum against `n_null` (new setting, default 1000) simulated companion-free searches, not a Šidák estimate over a nominal `(2 max_sep / (λ/B))²` trials. It is capped at `norm.isf(1/(n_null+1))` (3.09σ by default): a strong companion that read e.g. 12.5σ now reads `≥3.09σ`, flagged by the new `global_nsigma_is_lower_bound`; rank on `local_nsigma`. `n_trials` is dropped; `false_alarm_probability`, `n_null`, `n_null_above` and `global_nsigma_is_lower_bound` are added; `global_nsigma_method` and the `detection` messages change. `n_null` too small for `detection_sigma` raises `ValueError`. `detection_sigma` stays 3.0 (false-alarm probability 1.35e-3); runs cost `n_null` extra grid searches.
