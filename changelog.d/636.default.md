`orbit_cells.period_domain` now sets `P_cut = max(2 Δt_min, p_floor)`, with `Δt_min` over nights merged at `gap_days = 0.5` d; the within-night bound `P_night` is dropped from the orbit-cells path, which had excluded about half of the true periods of simulated systems. `PeriodDomain` reports the binding term, the inputs of `P_max` and the prior mass of the `N = 0` band. `NightSummary` stores the frame offsets and weights, and the orbit-cells likelihood predicts each night's `(p̂, v̂)` as the exact least-squares projection of the orbit at those offsets instead of the instantaneous state, which removes a curvature bias of up to about 15σ for eccentric short-period orbits. `period_domain` no longer takes `night_length`, `resolution` or `motion_fraction`.
