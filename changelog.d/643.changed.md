The leg-1 orbit search (`orbit_cells`, provisional) stops each set and C-step Newton at `|w_P| >= ln(1/δ)`, drops long-span candidates that run out of a cell towards a searched neighbour, and adds a neighbour's mode as a mixture component when its ρ-Laplace puts more than δ across the shared edge (draws stay per cell). `search_cell` reports `capped`, `newton_runs`, `edge_stopped` and `dropped` in `info`; `position_leg` takes `neighbours` and `shared`.
