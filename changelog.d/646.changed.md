Orbit population census (`scripts/orbit_population.py`): paired per-system seed streams (`SeedSequence([seed, i])`, a separate PIT stream), a full record for every system including failed ones (flagged-cell table, `p_true`, 99 % set, PITs labelled `pit_diagnostic`), strata (B″, ≤ m inliers, rest, ≤ 2 outlier nights), SBC ranks of a, e, cos i, Ω and ω, design §14 assertions 4–8, an `--oracle` mass check of the true cell, and `scripts/orbit_population_replay.py`. `orbit_cells` gains diagnostics only (capped Newton counts in `CellFit.info`, `CellsResult.upper` and `matters`, `truth_chart`, `oracle_evidence`); no fit result changes. The criteria are in `design/orbit_census_criteria.md`.
