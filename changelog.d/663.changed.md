Pipeline checks no longer hide failures. An unidentified `model_gain` warns instead of passing and a gain below −1 fails; `short_baselines` fails on a non-finite residual or error; `quoted_errors`, `orientation`, `edges`, `ensemble_kept` and `ensemble_spread` can fail on non-finite or empty inputs; `convergence` fails when the optimizer failed at every grid position. NUTS chain starts whose jittered log-density is not finite are redrawn instead of copying chain 0, and raise after 20 draws.
