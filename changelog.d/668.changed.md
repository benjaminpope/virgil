`fit(precondition="diag")` and `gauss_newton_diag`: when the diagonal is probed (above 1024 coordinates), coordinate blocks of at most 64 (gains, fluxes, positions) keep exact entries, up to 1024 exact coordinates in all; the large blocks keep the probe estimate, so the returned values change. A non-finite entry of a block's diagonal is now replaced by the block's largest finite entry with a `RuntimeWarning` (it was set to 0, which the floor turned into the widest step). Exact columns run in batches of 16.
