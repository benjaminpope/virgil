`position_cells`: a cell whose importance sampling is unreliable (ESS below `ESS_FAIL`) and that matters now gets its evidence and posterior draws from an adaptive tempered SMC that starts at the search-built defensive mixture (`orbit_cells.smc_cell`, design §6.15). Every other cell is unchanged. The census CSV gains `n_smc`, `n_smc_failed`, `smc_stages`, `smc_stages_max` and `smc_seconds`.

The SMC is now label-augmented: each particle carries its mixture component, and HMC moves it with that component's mass (the image-conjugated Laplace covariance, or the chart prior's variances for the prior share), with a Gibbs step redrawing the label, instead of one mass for the whole cloud (`tempered_smc(masses=…)`, `DefensiveMixture.log_components`, `masses` and `sample_labelled`). The posterior draws of every cell with modes use the same labelled resample-move, so they differ from earlier results (ln Ẑ does not). The SMC error is the largest of the genealogy, ESS-floor and replicate variances of four replicates (two at doubled mutation), and a mixing check flags a run whose doubled-mutation replicates sit significantly above the others (`SMCResult.log_z_floor_variance`, `log_z_rep_variance`, `mixing_z`, `steps_per_replicate`; `tempered_smc(steps=…)` takes one value per replicate).
