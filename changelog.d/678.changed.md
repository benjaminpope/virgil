`position_cells`: a cell whose importance sampling is unreliable (ESS below `ESS_FAIL`) and that matters now gets its evidence and posterior draws from an adaptive tempered SMC that starts at the search-built defensive mixture (`orbit_cells.smc_cell`, design §6.15). Every other cell is unchanged. The census CSV gains `n_smc`, `n_smc_failed`, `smc_stages`, `smc_stages_max` and `smc_seconds`.

The SMC is label-augmented: each particle carries its mixture component, and HMC moves it with that component's mass, with a Gibbs step redrawing the label (`tempered_smc(masses=…, pool=…)`, `DefensiveMixture.log_components`, `masses`, `pooling` and `sample_labelled`). Each stage sets a label's mass from the weighted covariance of its group's particles (the images of a mode pooled), tunes the step size and trajectory length per group, and runs HMC steps until the cloud has moved 90% of the distance between independent draws (`min_steps=5`, `max_steps=50`; reaching the cap sets `SMCResult.mixing_short`). `tempered_smc` and `bridge_smc` take `tempering_ess` (default 0.5, the conditional ESS that sets each β). The posterior draws of every cell with modes use the same labelled resample-move, so they differ from earlier results (ln Ẑ does not). The SMC error is the largest of the genealogy, ESS-floor and replicate variances of four replicates (`SMCResult.log_z_floor_variance`, `log_z_rep_variance`).

Known limitation: on a strongly curved target (the banana toy with a thin ridge) the late-stage HMC does not transport the tails, so the evidence is flagged (`mixing_short`) and slightly low (200 keys: 9 low misses, all flagged). The redesign's z/ζ/anchored-TI′ coordinates remove the curvature. The s1=0.2 case of the 200-key gate is a strict `xfail`.
