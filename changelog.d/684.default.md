The orbit-cell outlier density `b_k` integrates the uniform flux prior over the Gaussian `ln f` measurement error (`OutlierPrior.log_flux_box`, closed form with normal CDFs). A night with an observed `ln f > 0` now has a finite `b_k` instead of `-inf` and a `ValueError` in `position_leg`, and `b_k` is lower by up to ln 2 for peaks within about 2σ_f of either end of the flux range, which moves default evidences and outlier weights. The population census no longer truncates the simulated `ln f` noise and scores ω folded mod 180° so near-twins are right up to the orbit swap (ω → ω + π), enforced by a test.
