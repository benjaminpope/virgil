Pipeline checks that could not fail now do: the disk `laplace_pd` check fails on a non-positive-definite Laplace Hessian, the orbit `winner_flags` check fails on `no-valid-mode`, `alias_ambiguity` fails when every rival alias band crashed, and `bands_failed` fails when at least half the bands failed. The χ²/N and error-scale limits widen for small N.
