Tempering is now opt-in: the `temper` default of `fit_orbit_aliases` and `OrbitPipeline` is `False` (ruling D6; `temper=True` until 0.5). Tempering itself is unchanged. Orbit results with default settings will differ, and not only in confidence: with no factors estimated, (1) the short-arc sign gate and cut use untempered gaps and covariances, so on correlated channels they can cut the true cycle count; (2) the `"auto"` primary test and the `LABELS_FREE_NATS`/`RESOLVE_NATS` verdicts use untempered nats, so a disc is called resolved and labels decided more readily; (3) alias probabilities are more confident and posteriors narrower; (4) `AliasResult.temper_f` and `temper_blocks` are `None`, and the pipeline's `tempering` check and the "several channels without positions" warning no longer fire. Pass `temper=True` for data with correlated channels.
