`OIData.with_gains` and `gain_modes` propagate log-|V| nuisance modes to `VisibilityAmplitude` (`"visamp"`) and `TripleAmplitude` (`"t3amp"`) blocks in `OIData.extras` by default; pass `include_extras=False` for `vis`-only gains (required by `marginal_loglike` and `scales="marginal"` when both `vis` and amplitude extras are present).
