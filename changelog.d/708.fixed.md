`ClosureOffsets` keeps frame-spanning `modes` as dense columns whitened in O(F C) time after per-frame blocks instead of merging frames into a single O(F³ C) block, and scales the periodic wrap-penalty rows `2 sin²(Δ/2)` by `hypot(σ, σ_off)` at the nominal `widths` across `whitened_residuals`, `marginal_loglike` and `score_orbits` (with `scale_penalty=False` to opt out).
