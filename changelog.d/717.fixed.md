`DiskPipeline`: the geometry and ring fits no longer start a ring on the `r_in` prior bound, where the first node of their radius grids sits. Started there, the fit's flat coordinate squashed the gradient to nothing and the fit stalled on the bound, reporting convergence away from the maximum (the single-ring geometry of the injected test disk stopped at r = 1.27 mas with an indefinite Hessian; started half a half-fringe inside, it reaches r = 2.43 mas against the true 2.5, with a positive definite Hessian). A grid radius on `r_in` now starts half a half-fringe above it. A geometry parameter that still ends within 0.1% of its bound is reported by `at_bound` as `geometry.<name>`, with its own message; where that bound is active (the KKT condition: −log L keeps falling past it, by its gradient against its curvature over the gap), the parameter is held fixed and the other Laplace widths come from the Hessian's free block. The Laplace widths of the geometry and ring fits now need the whole Hessian, or its free block, to be positive definite: an indefinite Hessian gives no widths, even when the diagonal of its inverse is positive, and `laplace_pd` fails.
