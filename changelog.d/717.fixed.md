`DiskPipeline`: a parameter of the single-ring geometry fit within 0.1% of its prior bound (typically the radius on the `r_in` hole) is now reported by the `at_bound` check as `geometry.<name>`, with its own message. Where that bound is active (the KKT condition: −log L keeps falling past it, by its gradient against its curvature over the gap), the parameter is held fixed and the other Laplace widths come from the Hessian on the free subspace, so it no longer fails `laplace_pd` as a missing width. The Laplace widths of the geometry and ring fits now need the whole Hessian, or its free block, to be positive definite: an indefinite Hessian gives no widths, even when the diagonal of its inverse is positive, and `laplace_pd` fails.
