`log_evidence(priors=)` now adds the prior precision, `-∂² ln p` at the MAP (by autodiff), to the Hessian block of the non-latent parameters it integrates, e.g. a `Normal` prior. Before, a parameter set by its prior had too small a Hessian block, which inflated the evidence or gave `-inf`. Priors `fit` handles in a flat coordinate (`LogUniform`, `IsotropicInclination`), keep zero precision, so their evidence is unchanged.
