Adds `virgil.enable_compilation_cache` (provisional), an opt-in helper that turns on JAX's persistent compilation cache with thresholds suited to virgil's many small programs, so later processes skip most compilation; see the Performance page. The Laplace covariance (`regularized_inverse`) and the fit tolerances now run on the host in NumPy, removing about fifteen single-operation compiles from a cold `laplace_cov`.
