Pixel renderings of `GravityDarkenedStar` on a rotated grid (`Image.from_model(..., rotation_deg=...)`, `rotation_deg="grid"`) and under `Rotated(...)` changed: the star was drawn unrotated, shifted and shrunk (a point at ±90°). The in-plane blur of `ModulatedGaussianRim` and `MultipoleDisk` in the same renderings now follows the grid rotation too. Their analytic `model()` visibilities are unchanged; only pixelized versions, and fits to rotated lattices that use them, change. Unrotated grids and unrotated models are bit-identical.
