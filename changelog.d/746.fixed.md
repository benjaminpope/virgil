Pixel renderings of `GravityDarkenedStar` changed on a rotated grid (`Image.from_model(..., rotation_deg=θ)` with θ ≠ 0) and under `Rotated(...)`: the star was drawn unrotated, shifted and shrunk (a point at ±90°). The in-plane blur of `ModulatedGaussianRim` and `MultipoleDisk` in the same renderings now follows the grid rotation too. Their analytic `model()` visibilities are unchanged; only pixelized versions, and fits that use them, change. Unrotated grids and unrotated models are bit-identical.
