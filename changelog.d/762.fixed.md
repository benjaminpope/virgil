CLEAN restore and auto-mask outputs change (audit 724#11.1, 727#4.1). `restore` centred its kernel half a pixel off on even-sized images, shifting every restored component by (0.5, 0.5) pixels and lowering its peak by about 4 per cent; the kernel now sits on pixel `n // 2`, so odd sizes (all that `clean` accepts) are unchanged. `usemask="auto-multithresh"` now follows Kepley et al. (2020, Eqs. 1-4): the seed threshold is `median + max(noisethreshold × rms, sidelobethreshold × sidelobe × |peak|)` of the residual itself (it was the rms of the beam-smoothed residual, with the whole threshold capped at half the peak), seeded pixels are always masked, and regions grow above `lownoisethreshold × rms + median` of the beam-smoothed residual. Source-free noise maps no longer seed masks. A field with nothing above the seed threshold stops at `"threshold"` or `"nsigma"` if the whole-image peak is at or below the floor, and otherwise with `"no_progress"` (an `nsigma=3` noise field gives `"no_progress"`, with one `history` entry); it used to seed the brightest region. Masks, and hence components and `stop`, can change on any `auto-multithresh` run. The sidelobe term stays capped at half the peak (virgil#551).
