CLEAN restore and auto-mask outputs change (audit 724#11.1, 727#4.1). `restore` centred its kernel half a pixel off on even-sized images, shifting every restored component by (0.5, 0.5) pixels and lowering its peak by about 4 per cent; the kernel now sits on pixel `n // 2`, so odd sizes (all that `clean` accepts) are unchanged. `usemask="auto-multithresh"` now follows Kepley et al. (2020, Eqs. 1-4): the seed threshold is `max(noisethreshold × rms, sidelobethreshold × sidelobe × |peak|) + median` of the residual itself (it was the rms of the beam-smoothed residual, with the whole threshold capped at half the peak), and regions grow above `lownoisethreshold × rms + median`. Source-free noise maps no longer seed masks, and a field with nothing above the seed threshold now stops at the `threshold` or `nsigma` floor with one `history` entry, where it used to seed the brightest region. Masks, and hence components and `stop`, can change on any `auto-multithresh` run. The sidelobe term stays capped at half the peak (virgil#551).
