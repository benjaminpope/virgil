Tempering is now opt-in: the `temper` default of `fit_orbit_aliases` and `OrbitPipeline` is `False` (ruling D6; `temper=True` is opt-in until 0.5). Orbit results with default settings will differ: band evidences are no longer divided by the effective-channel factor, so alias probabilities are more confident and posteriors narrower on correlated channels. Tempering itself is unchanged.
