Follow-up to #665 (review of #585 findings 4, 6, 8, 9, 15). **Tolerances #665 loosened are tightened back, and what it changed is stated plainly.** (1) The refinement `dtol` in `epochs` and `night_summaries` was set to 1e-9 nats in #665, which loosened it (the old rescue accepted 10 ulp of Φ, which is below 1e-9 for |Φ| < 4.5e5); it is now `dtol=None`, the new kernel option meaning the rounding resolution of Φ itself (`_gauss_newton.rounding_dtol`, 10 ulp of max(|Φ|, 1), Higham 2002 §4.2), the same criterion the old rescue used. (2) The SVI `converged` rule now needs both the pre-#665 `drop <= 2 se` and `drop + 2 se <= 1 nat`, so it is never looser than before: #665's rule alone passed a 0.3-nat fall measured at 21 se. (3) The flux-grid convergence allowance uses the same rounding resolution, with its factor derived, and the float32 offset caveat (up to ~0.1σ at ℓ = -5000) restored. (4) Since #665, a kernel run that ends in the damping limit with no criterion met is `STALLED`, not `CONVERGED`, so it takes the L-BFGS fallback in `arcs`, `aliases` and `epochs`.
