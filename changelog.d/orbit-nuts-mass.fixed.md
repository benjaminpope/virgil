`OrbitPipeline`'s posterior stage no longer stalls on data with gains. NUTS now starts from the mode's Laplace covariance as a dense inverse mass matrix (eigenvalues clipped when the Hessian is not positive definite), with the chains jittered about the mode by a fraction of its width, and the tree depth is capped (`posterior_max_tree_depth`, 8). The stage has a wall-clock budget (`posterior_max_hours`, 12): a band that runs out of time reports `converged: false`, `budget_exhausted: true` and its step size, tree depth and gradient count, in place of a job timeout.
